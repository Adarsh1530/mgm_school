About Our School

Welcome to MGM Model School, Ayiroor

MGM Model School, Ayiroor, Varkala, founded in 1983, offers holistic CBSE education fostering 21st-century skills, responsibility, and creativity, preparing students for SSE and AISSCE while emphasizing ethics, innovation, and overall development.

Innovation Centre

Three digital libraries, a Maths 3D Zone, butterfly garden, and swimming pool foster diverse skills and practical learning experiences.

Modern Teaching Methods

Utilizes advanced tools and techniques for assessment, evaluation, and innovative pedagogy to enhance student learning and engagement.

World-Class Amenities

State-of-the-Art Innovation Centre & AI Robotics Hub

Inaugurated by the Hon'ble Governor of Kerala, Shri Arif Mohammed Khan, our Innovation Centre is equipped with cutting-edge technology and world-class amenities designed to spark curiosity, exploration, and 21st-century problem-solving skills.

OMNIS – The MGM Robot

Interactive AI-powered learning companion explaining complex concepts and making learning interactive.

NOVA – The AI Hub

KG Innovation Lab with Cubelets, block-based coding, and MIKO student-friendly robot.

3D Printing & Prototyping

Hands-on rapid prototyping allowing student engineering designs to materialize into physical models.

Virtual Reality (VR) Lab

Immersive experiential simulations across space astronomy, marine biology, and world geography.

National Distinction & Excellence

National Honors & Presidential Recognition

Celebrating historic benchmarks achieved by MGM Model School on prestigious national platforms.

Ministry of Jal Shakti

6th National Water Award 2024

MGM Model School, Ayiroor, Varkala has been conferred with a Special Mention – Best School/College Category at the 6th National Water Awards 2024 by the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India. Selected from 751 schools and colleges across India, the honour was presented at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi on 18th November 2025 in the august presence of Hon'ble President of India Smt. Droupadi Murmu. Principal Dr. Pooja S received the award from Hon'ble Union Minister Shri C. R. Paatil for our campus Rainwater Harvesting system and 36 conservation pits replenishing the groundwater table.

Selected from 751 schools & colleges across India • Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi (18 Nov 2025)
National Principal Award

Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan National Principal Award 2025

Conferred upon Principal Dr. Pooja S by Dr. Subramanyam Sharma, grandson of Bharat Ratna Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (former President of India), at the Grand Conclave held on 22nd August 2025 at Neo Convention, Janwada, Hyderabad. This esteemed national honour recognizes her visionary leadership, exceptional contributions to empowering 21st-century learners, transformative pedagogy, and elevating MGM Model School to new heights of excellence.

Grand Conclave, Neo Convention, Hyderabad (22 Aug 2025) • Visionary Leadership
