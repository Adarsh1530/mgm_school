NATIONAL WATER AWARD 2024 – A MOMENT OF NATIONAL PRIDE

MGM Model School, Ayiroor, Varkala has been conferred with a Special Mention – Best School/College Category at the 6th National Water Awards 2024 by the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India.

Selected from 751 schools and colleges across India, the prestigious honour was presented at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi on 18th November 2025, in the august presence of Hon'ble President of India Smt. Droupadi Murmu. 

Our Principal Dr. Pooja S received the award from Hon'ble Union Minister Shri C. R. Paatil. This national recognition is a testament to our sustained commitment to environmental sustainability and responsible resource management.

As part of our eco-friendly campus mission, the school has implemented an innovative Rainwater Harvesting system with rooftop collection units and 36 rainwater conservation pits across the campus. This system helps replenish the groundwater table, prevents flooding, and ensures water availability for irrigation and well-recharge even during dry spells. 

A proud moment for the entire MGM family and a small step towards a greener, water-secure future!

VRIKSHA CHIKITSA – HEALING NATURE THE AYURVEDIC WAY

In the year 2024, MGM Model School, Ayiroor, Varkala took a unique step towards environmental conservation by organizing a "Vriksha Chikitsa" – Traditional Ayurvedic Tree Treatment project in our campus. 

The program was led by renowned tree healer and environmental activist Shri. K. Binu, popularly known as Vriksha Vaidyan or 'Tree Doctor'. The Kerala-based Tree Doctor enlightened our students about the meticulous process involved in the preparation of Ayurvedic medicines for healing injured trees. As part of this green initiative, we invited Shri. Binu and his team to treat and rejuvenate our beloved jackfruit tree, aged over 40 years, standing tall in our campus. The ancient Ayurvedic healing ritual gave our children a rare and inspiring lesson on compassion, conservation and co-existing with nature.

20-20 METHOD OF TEACHING

At MGM Model School, true education is not about imposing knowledge, but about nurturing the power of thought in every student. Our innovative Twenty-20 method redefines the 40-minute period into 20 minutes for the teacher and 20 minutes for the students. The next day's topic is given in advance, so students come prepared. 

Each day, 5 students present their understanding in 4 minutes each, ensuring every child gets a chance to think, speak and lead. The best presentations are appreciated and encouraged, building confidence, communication skills, critical thinking and independent learning.

3D MATHS ZONE - Where Maths Comes to Life

Our outdoor 3D Maths Zone turns abstract concepts into a hands-on experience. 

At MGM Model School, we believe learning is best when it can be seen, touched and experienced. Set within the school campus, this interactive zone features large, vividly coloured 3D models of solid shapes including Triangular Pyramid, Square Pyramid, Pentagonal Pyramid, Hexagonal Pyramid, Cubes, Rectangular Prisms, Frustum of a Cone, Cones and more. 

Each model is mounted on a pedestal that clearly displays its properties — Faces, Vertices, Edges, and the Euler's formula (F+V-E), along with Lateral Surface Area, Total Surface Area and Volume — making learning spontaneous and engaging. 

Instead of just memorizing formulas from a textbook, students walk through geometry, observe, count and correlate. It fosters spatial intelligence, logical reasoning and a genuine love for Mathematics from the foundational years.

NCC (NATIONAL CADET CORPS)

MGM Model School launched its NCC unit - '1 Kerala Battalion, Kerala Lakshadweep Group' on 7th July 2022 with cadets from the Junior Wing (Class VIII).

Functioning as a self-financing scheme with 4 years of service, the unit provides comprehensive training every Friday from 3:00 PM to 5:30 PM including basic military parades, small arms training, drills and community service. Cadets also participate in camps and expeditions that develop leadership, resilience and a deep sense of patriotism. 

On successful completion of 2 years, cadets are awarded the NCC Certificate - a recognition of their dedication and discipline, which adds significant value to their future academic and career pursuits.
