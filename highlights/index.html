National Recognition 18th November 2025

National Water Award 2024 – A Moment of National Pride

MGM Model School, Ayiroor, Varkala has been conferred with a Special Mention – Best School/College Category at the 6th National Water Awards 2024 by the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India. Selected from 751 schools across India, the honour was presented at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi in the august presence of Hon'ble President of India Smt. Droupadi Murmu. Principal Dr. Pooja S received the award from Hon'ble Union Minister Shri C. R. Paatil.

Features 36 rainwater conservation pits and rooftop harvesting replenishing campus groundwater.

Our beloved Principal Dr. Pooja S was honoured with the prestigious Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan National Principal Award 2025. The award was presented by Dr. Subramanyam Sharma, grandson of Bharat Ratna Dr. S. Radhakrishnan, former President of India, at the Grand Conclave held in Hyderabad, in recognition of her visionary leadership and unwavering commitment to delivering quality education.

20-20 Method of Teaching

At MGM Model School, true education is not about imposing knowledge, but about nurturing the power of thought in every student. Our innovative Twenty-20 method redefines the 40-minute period into 20 minutes for the teacher and 20 minutes for the students. The next day's topic is given in advance, so students come prepared.
