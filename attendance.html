Attendance

Attendance

Every student must have compulsory 75% attendance in an academic session. Medical certificate or other necessary documents must be produced to the school authorities for the leave already availed Leave shall be granted only when it is absolutely necessary or on medical grounds. Leave should be marked on the stipulated page in the Diary. Student shall not be allowed to attend the class without leave application on the day he/she presents himself/herself after leave. Students with perfect attendance (100%) will be honoured with certificates on the Convocation day
