Our Founder

Our Founder

MGM Model School, a flagship project of Aikya Pradayini Trust, was founded in 1983 by a group of dedicated social workers with a vision to provide quality education. Under the leadership of Dr. P.k. Sukumaran, a renowned educationist and philanthropist, the school has grown exponentially from humble beginnings with just 5 students to a thriving institution with an impressive student body of around 2700. Dr. Sukumaran’s tireless efforts and commitment to excellence have been instrumental in shaping the school’s journey, transforming it into a centre of excellence that nurtures young minds and empowers them to reach their full potential.
