Admission Procedure

Parents/guardians register their child's name and details in the school Website / office.

Shortlisted students and parents will be called to verify documents on a specified date

After verification, willing parents submit the application form and required documents, agreeing to the school's rules, regulations, and fee structure.

Important Notes

  • Once fees are paid, no refunds will be made for discontinuation or expulsion due to indiscipline.
  • Application for Transfer Certificate (TC) should be submitted 10 days in advance.
