Voice Of MGM

On Air • Campus Broadcasting

"Voice of MGM" – Our School Radio Club

The "Voice of MGM" Radio Club provides an authentic broadcasting platform for students to cultivate self-assurance, develop polished public speaking skills, and broadcast creative stories across our campus community.

Campus Radio Broadcasting Station Student sound engineers and radio jockeys in live broadcast
Acoustic Recording & Podcast Lab Professional broadcast microphones and digital mixing consoles
Confidence Building

Unlike intimidating stage performances, speaking into an acoustic studio microphone allows children to express themselves naturally and freely.

Public Speaking

Mastering pronunciation, tone modulation, cadence, and eloquence to communicate impactful narratives with clarity and poise.

Content Scripting

Writing campus bulletins, cultural reviews, science explainers, and poetry to hone journalistic research and storytelling flair.

Team Collaboration

Managing live cue sheets, coordinating guest speaker interviews, and collaborating as a unified production crew.

Inaugurated by Press Information Bureau Leadership

The club was officially inaugurated by Smt. Rashmi Roja Thushara Nair IAS, Bureau Director of the Press Information Bureau (PIB). Her encouragement marked a milestone for student media and democratic voice expression at MGM Model School.
