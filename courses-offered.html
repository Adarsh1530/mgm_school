Courses Offered

Our school, following the NCERT syllabus, prepares students for the All India Secondary School Examination (AISSE) and the Senior School Certificate Examination (SSCE) conducted by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE).

The medium of instruction is English, and the curriculum includes:

Academic Calendar & Facilities

  • Week-long breaks during Onam and Christmas, summer vacation in April-May and restricted holidays on public holidays only.
  • Smart classrooms with state-of-the-art technology.
  • Internet browsing facilities for enhanced learning.
  • Well-stocked libraries: one in the Kindergarten section and three digital libraries in Primary, Secondary and Senior Secondary sections.
  • Practical learning tools: 3D Maths Zone, Globe and over 10 labs.
